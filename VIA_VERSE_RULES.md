# Via Verse: Reference & Rules

Personal daily newsletter for Vojeswitha. Read time is 10 to 15 minutes, never more.

## Delivery
- Arrives **before 7:00 AM IST** every day (the routine runs at about 6:15 AM).
- Sent from her Gmail to vojeswitha@gmail.com and vojeswithag@gmail.com.
- **Subject:** fresh each day, always naming Via Verse, built around the quote person and the weekday.
  - Example: `Via Verse · A Marie Curie edge to your Wednesday`
- **Srija copy** (srijamuvva03@gmail.com): a separate copy with **all CAT content removed** (no countdown, calendar, focus chips or quiz) that greets "Good morning, Srija." Never send it without approval.
- Never send to anyone else without approval or a draft card.

## What to include, in order
1. **Header and greeting:** the Baymax image (armoured red Baymax and Hiro at sunset, flipped so Baymax is on the right).
2. **CAT countdown:** a one-month calendar marking exam day (29 Nov), the days remaining, and progress. It sits directly before the day's goals. The CAT goal text is large.
3. **Current focus / goals:** large, bold text. Background is McQueen racing, rendered across the block with the whole car visible.
4. **Quote of the day:** the person's photo (influential women, past or present) top-left, with the quote text continuing under it. Background is the user's Naruto image: head sharp and never cut, left and right edges blurred in straight bands, no radial or round fade.
5. **Exactly three picks:**
   - **What experts say:** a top founder or leader article. Background is Po under the cherry tree.
   - **Startup study:** a real startup case study covering how it started, grew and makes money. Rotate sectors.
   - **Finance / current news / markets** and **AI learning:** one pick each, so the three picks are one startup/founder pick, one finance pick and one AI pick.
   - Backgrounds: WALL·E at night, Remy (Ratatouille), Cars.
6. **CAT vocab quiz:** at the very bottom, with the answers and each word's meaning right under it. Background is the study girl, filling the block, with no blur.

**Do NOT include** an "In today's issue" outline or agenda, or anything beyond the items above.

## Links
- Use only direct publisher URLs, each checked to open. **Never** Google News redirect links.

## Visual style
- A light theme: white or near-white background with soft pastel light-blue section blocks, soft borders and pale accents. Never dark or cluttered.
- Roboto Mono font.
- Keep the older pastel email layout, not the numbered/TL;DR redesign.
- **No white panels** behind text. Use darker, bolder text, a light text-shadow, and a slight soft blur only under the text.
- Background images are strong and colourful, never washed out.

## Image rules (most important)
- Each background fills its block. The character is **fully in frame** on the image's empty side, never cut, especially heads.
- **Laptop and phone use the SAME photo** per block, as wide and tall versions of one artwork, each cropped and zoomed **separately**. Never apply one global rule.
- **Phone:** the main character is always on the **right**.
- **Laptop:** WALL·E at the far right, Po towards the right, Naruto at the far right of the quote block.
- Remy must be clearly visible.
- Use a designer's judgement: check every render on laptop and phone before sending. No radial gradients.
- Images are hosted in the public repo `vojeswitha/viaverse-newsletter` under `img/`. Every version gets new filenames so caches never serve old images.
- Commit once and check the commits page.
- Separate laptop and phone layouts, with a phone `@media (max-width:480px)` rule swapping in the `ph*` images.
